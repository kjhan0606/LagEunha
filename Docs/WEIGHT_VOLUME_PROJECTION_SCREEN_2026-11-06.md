# Kinematic volume projection screen (not adopted)

New offline candidate, distinct from the retired psi-times-strain R model.
Uses existing native 32x32 face geometry at initial Gresho and t=.1 frozen/
dynamic states. No production force law, time evolution or weight bound was
changed. Python/SciPy is used only for algebraic diagnostics.

## Definition

Each unique oriented face has length A and generator distance d. D maps its
normal velocity to signed cell volume rate with entries +A,-A. N maps cell
translation velocities to the exact geometric normal face velocity at fixed
weights. K maps Wdot to (Wdot_i-Wdot_j)/(2d). Hence B=DN and L=DK.

T maps cell velocity values to the average of two locally linearly
reconstructed face velocities, dotted with the normal. Each cell gradient
uses neighbor inverse-distance-squared least squares, without a fit parameter.
This is a smooth-flow screen, not shock-limited reconstruction.

Let C=DT. Solve L Wdot=(C-B)v, with zero mean weight rate. Then actual
geometric volume rate Bv+L Wdot=Cv. This is also the normal equation of the
face-velocity least-squares fit with face norm diag(A*d): K^T H K=L/2 and
K^T H=D/2. The mean-zero compatibility condition is satisfied for closed
periodic geometry. Individual face velocities generally remain unmatched.

The compatible pressure force is C^T P and Udot=-P Cv. Its force evaluation
is local via T and D and need not apply L inverse; evolving W still requires
a graph solve. No independent W momentum or auxiliary kinetic energy is used.
This is a necessary power pairing, not a proof of physical adequacy.

## Native-state results

Neighbor identities are independently recovered from power-plane/orientation
relations, requiring squared residual <1e-18 and reciprocal neighbors. Max
plane/orientation mismatch is3.42e-13. Degenerate edges below1e-12 length are
omitted for diagnostics. The candidate uses actual exported native faces.

Volume constraint max residual <=3.68e-17; total power residual <1e-16.
Uniform-pressure force <1.56e-17. Largest acoustic frequency ratio C/B at a
uniform-pressure rest linearization is1.567 initial,1.541 deformed frozen,
1.522 deformed dynamic. These are numerical eigensolves, not certified bounds
or end-to-end runtime measurements. They do not show the prior36x pathology.

Use analytic Gresho pressure averaged within each actual cell, and independently
integrate its surface traction with48-point line quadrature. Volume-weighted
L1 acceleration error against that integrated traction is:

| geometry | native B | candidate C |
|---|---:|---:|
| initial | .0241294 | .0131735 |
| deformed frozen | .0312413 | .0374734 |
| deformed dynamic | .0428964 | .0394628 |

Pressure cell averages for deformed cells are the previously converged12/24/48
triangle quadrature; initial pressure uses the existing IC quadrature.
The surface reference uses48 points but its own order-refinement check has
not yet been completed. These force comparisons are frozen-state tests, not
predictions of a trajectory generated by C. They remove the ambiguity of
comparing cell-averaged force to point centripetal acceleration alone.

Face normal velocity mismatch RMS after the fit is .003559,.004758,.005327
for the three actual velocity fields. Matching net volume is not matching
every material face. The weight admissibility bound is not enforced by this
unconstrained graph solve; no claim of solving the KH stop is made.

## Decision

Do not launch a new full PDE or choose this as default from its improved
initial result. Its deformed-cell force improvement is mixed, individual
material faces remain unmatched, shock entropy and 1D Riemann reduction are
unqualified, and a graph solve has an unmeasured production cost. Preserve
this screen as a kinematic alternative with explicit force/work pairing;
no empirical patch, extra inertia or relaxed .9 bound was added.
