# Anchored graph and volume-solver reuse, 2026-11-09

This is a computational candidate for the existing geometric volume equation. It adds no physical term, changes no face coefficient or RHS, and retains the original raw-face residual threshold, nonlinear volume tests and thermal/geometry acceptance checks. The live fixed-wall Cylinder continuation keeps its original executable. The first astronomy draft retains priority; no second manuscript is started.

## Need and construction

The saved 50-step current-run profile is `graph_lu_reuse_v1/current_runtime_profile.json`. Of 167.559 accepted-step seconds, 62.601 are inclusive volume linear work, 61.2563 the full tessellation path, and 32.4342 acceleration. All 100 linear solves have a changed positive CSR pattern. Consequently, simply connecting the previously qualified identical-pattern LU cache cannot accelerate this interval.

The current equation is Jx = a_i*x_i + sum_j c_ij*(x_i-x_j), with c_ij>=0 and a_i the positive prescribed-exterior coefficient. A reverse BFS from anchored rows verifies that every row has a directed positive-coefficient path to an anchor. The maximum-absolute-value null-vector argument then proves structural nonsingularity of this raw operator, including the nonsymmetric case. Self faces and exactly zero coefficients contribute nothing; no epsilon is introduced. This is not a condition-number or current-factor pivot certificate.

Ordered material IDs and anchor rows still invalidate old factors when changed. When the current graph passes, old LU is only a right preconditioner for the current raw-J GMRES. At most the pre-existing eight Krylov iterations are tried, then current symbolic/numeric factorization is used on failure. `same_pattern` remains separate from permission to precondition: changed-pattern fallback cannot copy new coefficients into an old CSR. The current residual limit remains 1e-10. This certificate covers anchored systems; a closed/periodic domain still requires a gauge and compatibility checks.

- In native 2D, stage volume accounting and inverse activation remain Cylinder-only. General periodic 2D needs the existing immutable-ID volume targets and original full-row residual, plus component compatibility and gauge removal. Subtracting the RHS mean would change the target and is not an acceptable unreported shortcut.

- The successful Kepler baseline is preserved. It provides evidence for matching geometry and pressure work; its success does not validate the new shock closure or this numerical reuse candidate.
- Matched physical accuracy, IC/BC, final time and controlled resources are still required to demonstrate total runtime no greater than twice GIZMO. Neither this local speedup nor the earlier differently qualified KH timing satisfies that requirement.

This is an idempotence defect of repeated clipping, not evidence of physical cell growth or a new constitutive term.
