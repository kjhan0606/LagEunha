# Native pressure-force audit of the unseeded shear disturbance

Source is the existing64x128 unseeded W=0 t=1 checkpoint from
KH_UNSEEDED_SHEAR_2026-11-06.md. No original checkpoint was changed.
Four copies were evaluated by the actual native C/CUDA periodic K1 path:
original, P=10 everywhere, P replaced by20-P, and vx=0 with vy retained.
Pressure counterfactuals change U consistently with stored V and native gamma;
they are diagnostic resets, not conservative updates or proposed corrections.
Positions, masses and geometry are identical across the four K1 evaluations.

## Measured forces

Original acceleration RMS is0.00169348 in x and0.00429550 in y.
Uniform-pressure acceleration RMS is2.87e-14 in x and2.85e-14 in y.
Reversing the pressure perturbation reverses acceleration, maximum residual
of a_reverse+a_original-2*a_uniform is2.85e-13.
Setting vx=0 leaves both acceleration components exactly unchanged.

Thus there is no evidence here of a constant-pressure force imbalance on
the distorted mesh or an explicit velocity-dependent force in this path.
The nonzero instantaneous force is generated by the pressure perturbation.
This does not certify that the pressure perturbation itself arose correctly,
nor that the spatial operator has correct short-wave dynamics.

At this one instant sum(m vy ay)=-5.29348e-8: the force is reducing transverse
kinetic energy, even though its longer-term envelope grows. It would be
incorrect to infer an always anti-damping force from the trajectory alone.
The x kinetic power is -3.44232e-7. Pressure/kinetic exchange must be tracked
over evolution rather than treating either instantaneous sign as instability.

## Further control

A distinct counterfactual evolution removes vx at t=1, retains original x,y,
mass,U,vy, and uses the same stricter Courant/geometric limits as the recent
refinement. It targets1.5. Removing vx removes its kinetic energy; conservation
is measured against this explicitly modified initial state only. It is not
an allowed production step and is not evidence for a physical damping model.
Its purpose is to distinguish evolution of the existing pressure disturbance
at near rest from evolution in the continuing shear.

This control completed t=1.5. Modified-state relative energy error is2.25e-14
and individual masses are unchanged. Comparing the same starting disturbance:

|case|transverse kinetic energy|relative pressure RMS|
|---|---:|---:|
|shared t=1|5.20366e-9|2.44902e-5|
|shear retained, refined steps, t=1.5|0.0148445|0.0500214|
|shear removed, t=1.5|3.46897e-9|2.36372e-5|

The existing pressure disturbance therefore stays small without continued
shear but amplifies strongly with shear. This narrows the issue to a coupling
in the evolving shear state rather than spontaneous pressure-force failure
of this near-rest state. It does not by itself separate physical shear
instability from mesh-scale numerical amplification; a continuum or resolved
linear-mode reference is still needed. The previously measured dominant
two-cell wavelength makes that separation essential. No production damping,
pressure reset, velocity reset, or new physical term is adopted.
