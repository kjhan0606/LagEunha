# Local orbital caps in the accepted eta=.06 checkpoint

At t=0.4125 the existing native rule is `eta*(r^2+epsilon^2)^0.75` with eta=.06 and epsilon=.01175. For all 90,352 current sites its global minimum is 7.64202e-5. Binned by original radius, the core has 134 sites and median local orbital cap 1.33232e-3, while the 48,680 disk sites with 0.5<=r0<2 have median local orbital cap 0.105106, or 1,375 times the global orbital minimum. The accepted restart hash and all regional summaries are in `LOCAL_ORBITAL_CAPS.json`.

These are orbital caps at a single state only. They are neither the complete hydrodynamic timestep limit nor a benchmark. The idealized independent-site work fraction in the JSON excludes shared faces, conservative paired impulses and work, wakeups, moving geometry, MPI synchronization and other physical constraints; it must not be quoted as an actual speedup. In particular, the earlier t10 analysis showed that small pressure acceleration does not imply slow compressional work. The result identifies why the global central timestep is expensive and motivates, but does not validate, conservative individual or multirate integration after the physical scheme is established.
