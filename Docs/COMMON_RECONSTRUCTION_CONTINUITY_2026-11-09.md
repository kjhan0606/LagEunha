# Common reconstruction continuity investigation — 2026-11-09

## Measured structural issue

The frozen common scalar projector uses face-measure-weighted least squares but imposes hard gradient bounds along every neighboring generator displacement. A newly created face with arbitrarily small positive length therefore has vanishing least-squares weight yet introduces a finite hard constraint. The resulting single cell gradient can change all of the cell's other face states. Multiplying both sides of a hard inequality by face length would NOT resolve this, because any positive common factor cancels.

`common_acoustic2d_20261109_retry1/audit_vanishing_face.py` constructs exact Bravais-lattice Voronoi cells in a periodic parallelogram. The lattice shear approaches zero from both signs; wavevector is transformed consistently, and cell-average density, pressure and momentum are independently integrated. It calls the unchanged frozen C scalar projector and free-RHS functions. This is an instantaneous manufactured-field experiment at32², not another hydrodynamic trajectory or a change to any live state.

At exactly zero shear the square cells have4faces. At shear−1e−8 there are6faces, with the two new faces only1.41421e−8 times the normal cell spacing. The raw least-squares density-gradient difference from the square case is4.2892e−8 when normalized by wave amplitude, but the PROJECTED gradient difference remains.591928. It approaches the same finite value across shear−1e−4,−1e−6,−1e−8. The normalized force change remains.00421758 instead of vanishing. Volume-rate relative L1 changes from.0243868 (square) to.0448596 (negative infinitesimal shear). The opposite sign tends continuously to the square result for this wave orientation. Files: `vanishing_face_audit/report.json` and all raw geometric/gradient/RHS arrays.

Thus at fixed resolution the current common scalar reconstruction has a one-sided discontinuity when the neighbor graph changes through a vanishing face. This is directly reproduced, but it is NOT a proof that it causes all evolved-wave error, Sedov anisotropy, Kepler heating or every prior failure. Continuum convergence as resolution increases is a different question; do not equate this diagnostic alone with failure of all conservation or hydrodynamics.

## Required property of a replacement

A face whose measure tends to zero must not continue to change unrelated finite-face exchanges through a hard global gradient constraint. Retain the existing weighted least-squares geometric fit, common pressure/force/work relation, fixed masses and dimensional algebra. Preserve positive Riemann inputs, constant-pressure contacts, smooth affine fields, shared equal/opposite face exchange, and the existing shock/rarefaction validation. The same construction must apply to1D/2D/3D and C/CUDA; no wave-, vortex- or shock-specific switches, new fitted coefficients, or mesh-size thresholds.

A minimal candidate to investigate is to replace the cell-wide active-set gradient clipping with a face-local reconstruction limiter, reusing the existing parameter-free ordering logic where appropriate. The raw geometric fit already loses the influence of a vanishing face continuously. A correction confined to that face would then have a vanishing flux contribution rather than changing every other face. Positivity and affine-field preservation on skew cells must be derived and tested, not assumed. Simple clamping to the two owner values can incorrectly limit a valid tangential linear gradient and is NOT automatically acceptable. Likewise, merely turning off projection to pass the wave test is not an acceptable adoption path.

The existing ordered-jump helper is dimension-independent, but its suitability for thermodynamic traces and pressure-offset reconstruction is not yet established. No replacement has been implemented or adopted. Before time evolution: test the vanishing-face limits, affine and constant states, pressure-offset balance, positivity and equal/opposite force/work. Then use the same candidate in the existing1D contacts/shocks/rarefactions and2D waves/shear/Gresho, followed by the broader2D suite. Keep original results and successful Kepler baseline intact. Measure cost and number of operations as well as accuracy; any simplification must contribute toward the still-unproven ≤2x GIZMO runtime objective.


## Isolated face-local candidate, first implementation and checks

Implemented only in `common_acoustic2d_20261109_retry1/face_local_candidate_v1` and `face_local_candidate_v2`. Production headers, executables, and all ongoing evolutions were NOT changed. The candidate retains the frozen face-measure weighted LS fit and pressure-acceleration effective displacements, removes the cell-global scalar gradient QP, and prepares paired thermodynamic traces with a dimension-independent scalar helper.

For owner means qi,qj and raw face traces Li,Rj, define the mean jump d0=qj-qi and reconstructed jump d=Rj-Li. Clamp d into [min(0,d0),max(0,d0)] to get dc; apply equal and opposite corrections (d-dc)/2 to Li,Rj. Thus the shared reconstructed face mean is retained while the jump cannot reverse sign or increase relative to the owner jump. A single face-local convex blend back to the positive owner means ensures positive traces using the existing 4096-epsilon arithmetic margin. This introduces neither a new physical variable nor a tunable physical coefficient.

Why not simply reuse the existing velocity alpha on both scalar increments? When owner means are equal but a tangential affine field gives the same nonzero face increment, a tiny roundoff difference in the two increments can force that alpha to zero and erase the legitimate common face value. Direct jump clamping avoids this common-mode loss. The pair helper itself contains no dimension-specific operation; raw fits and geometry adapters still need dimensional validation.

The vanishing-face manufactured-wave test now changes normalized force continuously from about7.16e-3 to7.16e-5,7.16e-7,7.16e-9 as negative lattice shear decreases from1e-2 to1e-8. Previously it tended to a nonzero~4.22e-3. The positive-shear limit is also continuous. These are frozen CPU instantaneous free-RHS tests, not an evolved wave or a full-method accuracy result. Velocity reconstruction remains unchanged; this does not prove full arbitrary-state operator continuity.

The first arithmetic implementation formed the left trace by subtracting half the jump from the mean. At a density contrast~2e11, cancellation corrupted the positivity blend: scaling all states changed a large output by relative2.33e-6. That failed version and its report are retained. V2 instead adds the jump correction directly to each original trace, preserving unmodified traces exactly. The same20,000 randomized positive-owner tests spanning1e-6..1e6 then gave max scaling error1.08e-15 and exact left/right exchange symmetry; final traces stayed positive and jumps stayed ordered to8.05e-16 relative roundoff.

For4000 manufactured face pairs in each of1D,2D,3D, positive affine face traces were preserved to2.19e-16 relative and paired grad(P)/rho pressure continuity to roundoff. These validate the pair algebra given exact gradients, NOT a complete multidimensional LS stencil, wall handling or a time integrator. Reconstructed free-RHS total energy derivatives cancel below3e-21 on the wave samples after converting the adapter H=Edot-v.F+P.Vdot back to Edot. Sum(H) alone is not energy conservation.

**Not adopted.** Jump ordering and positivity do not bound the common mode: owner values(1,2), raw traces(3,4) retain(3,4). Therefore no cell-average maximum principle, shock monotonicity, entropy stability or full positivity-preserving time integration is established. The next gate is the existing1D contact/shock/rarefaction suite with this same face construction, followed by2D waves, shear and Gresho; wall and CUDA parity and cost measurements are also outstanding. Do not add a problem-specific sensor to conceal a failure. No new3D hydrodynamic run has been started.


## Nine-case1D gate and face-envelope replacement

The first paired jump/positivity candidate (`face_local_candidate_v2`, exercised by `face_local_1d_v1`) was **rejected as a production replacement**. All9cases reached the unchanged physical endpoints with fixed masses and energy balance, but Blast density cell-mean L1 rose from0.04110979 to0.28072203 and colliding shocks from0.00345492 to0.09169198. Contact preservation and conserved total energy therefore did not establish accuracy. Full failed-candidate outputs, comparisons and figures remain saved. These tests used the existing `Exam/HydroCore/material1d_common.c` driver and exact Riemann solver, SSPRK2, unchanged CFL0.3,200 physical cells except Shu–Osher800; source modifications were isolated in the research directory. Initial data, padding, boundaries, final times, velocity limiter and force/work were unchanged.

`face_local_candidate_v3` replaces the positivity-only common-mode treatment with a face-local scalar interval. For each owner i, translate its mean tangentially to the evaluation point: ti=qi+gi·[ri−n(n·ri)]. Define an interval spanning qi,qj,ti,tj, intersected with positivity at the existing4096-epsilon roundoff scale. Keep the ordered jump dc and clamp the lower of the reconstructed pair into [lo,hi−|dc|]; add |dc| to obtain the other trace. No cell-wide constraint or new physical coefficient is introduced. Including the unshifted positive owners guarantees a nonempty admissible interval and |dc| within its width. On a genuine shared face separating the cell centroids, an affine field's exact face value lies between its tangentially translated means, so a constant or affine field is preserved. For1D the tangential offsets vanish and this becomes a two-owner interval. This is one reconstruction rule across dimensions, not a shock-specific switch. It does not itself prove an entropy inequality or a time-discrete maximum principle.

Important test qualification: earlier pair-algebra affine samples allowed an arbitrary point beyond the two owners, useful for the unrestricted mean but not a physical1D shared face. V3 tests use shared planes separating the centroids, arbitrary skew tangential displacements, and positive affine traces,4000cases per dimension. These and grad(P)/rho equilibrium face continuity passed.20,000 random positive-owner pairs passed positivity/order/scaling to3.4e-16 relative roundoff.
