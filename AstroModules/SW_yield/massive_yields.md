## Key Observations

### 1. Metallicity Dependence of Wind Mass Loss

| Mass | Z=0.02 | Z=0.002 | Z=0.0002 | Z=0.00002 |
|-----:|-------:|--------:|---------:|----------:|
| 25 M☉ | 16.0 | 6.65 | 1.98 | 2.86 |
| 60 M☉ | 42.1 | 27.2 | 14.3 | 11.4 |
| 120 M☉ | 94.1 | 74.4 | 35.8 | 26.1 |

**Wind mass loss ∝ Z^(0.5-0.8)** (강한 metallicity 의존성)

### 2. Yield Sign Patterns

| Element | Solar Z | Low Z |
|:--------|:-------:|:-----:|
| H | **−** (consumed) | **−** |
| He | **+** (produced from H) | **+** |
| C | **+** (mixing brings He-burning product) | **+** |
| N | **+** (CNO cycle product) | **+** |
| O | **−** (CNO cycle consumptio) | **+** (at low Z) |
| Z_total | **+** (>20 M☉) | **+** |

### 3. Physical Interpretation

- **Solar Z:** 강한 wind → He-burning 산물(C)이 표면에 도달 → 양수 C, Z yield
- **Low Z:** 약한 wind → 주로 CNO processed material → N 증가, C/O 변환
- **Very low Z:** 매우 약한 wind → 거의 변화 없음
